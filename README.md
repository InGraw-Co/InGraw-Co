# Hey, I'm Mateusz! 👋

I'm a young electronics enthusiast, embedded systems developer, and programmer from Poland. I'm also the founder of **InGraw Co.**

I love designing things from scratch, especially when hardware and software come together. From custom PCBs and handheld consoles to Linux-based single-board computers, I enjoy understanding how everything works — not just making it work.

Over the years, I've designed my own electronic devices, including multilayer PCBs and ARM-based computers. I've worked with SoCs such as the Allwinner H616, dealing with DDR4 initialization, bootloaders, eMMC booting, Linux kernels, device trees, drivers, and plenty of hardware debugging.

Getting a computer I designed myself to boot Linux and run a graphical desktop was one of my biggest achievements.

## 🛠️ Skills & Technologies

- **Programming & Scripting:** C++, Python, GDScript, Shell, HTML *(primarily AI-agent development)*
- **Hardware:** PCB design, ARM SoCs, microcontrollers, DDR memory, eMMC, electronics troubleshooting
- **Embedded Linux:** U-Boot, Buildroot, Linux kernel, device trees, drivers, Wayland, Qt
- **Interfaces:** SPI, I²C, UART, USB, HDMI
- **Tools:** EasyEDA, VS Code, Unreal Engine 5, Godot, Blender, Affinity, Git
- **Other:** 3D modeling, 3D printing, game development, UI design

## 🧠 A Bit About Me

I'm fascinated by how computers work at every level — from electrical signals and processor architecture to operating systems and graphical interfaces.

I learn best by building real things, experimenting, making mistakes, and figuring out why something doesn't work.

Sometimes that means spending days debugging a single issue. Sometimes it means accidentally adding another component to my personal **Museum of Disasters**. 💀

I prefer quality over cutting corners, and I believe that even an unsuccessful project is worth something if I learn from it.

My approach to engineering is simple: **understand it, build it, break it, fix it, and make it better.**
